🧭 ORID & Pyramid Principle Guide

ORID helps transform observations into decisions. The Pyramid Principle helps communicate those decisions clearly. Together they form a complete thinking and communication framework.

ORID Framework

O — Objective

Focus on facts, observations and evidence. Avoid opinions or conclusions.

  • What happened?
  • What data exists?
  • What did I observe?

R — Reflective

Capture reactions, surprises, concerns and intuition.

  • What surprised me?
  • What excited me?
  • What concerned me?

I — Interpretive

Identify patterns, root causes and implications.

  • Why did this happen?
  • What patterns exist?
  • What lessons can be learned?

D — Decisional

Convert learning into action.

  • What should happen next?
  • What action should be taken?
  • Who owns it?

📚 Example

Objective
Project slipped by 2 weeks.

Reflective
Team was frustrated by requirement changes.

Interpretive
Lack of change control created rework.

Decisional
Create weekly change-review meetings.

Recommendation
Implement a formal change control process.

✅ Golden Rule

Observe objectively.
Reflect honestly.
Interpret thoughtfully.
Decide deliberately.
Communicate clearly.
