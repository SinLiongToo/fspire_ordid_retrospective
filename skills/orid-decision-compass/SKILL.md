# ORID Mindset & Decision Compass Skill

This skill guides the design, development, and application of the **ORID Mindset & Decision Compass** — a multi-dimensional reflection and structured decision-making framework.

## 🧘 Stage 0: Mindful Grounding (Pre-Reflection)
Before engaging in deep logical analysis or emotional reflection, calibrate the autonomic nervous system:
- **Box Breathing (4-4-4-4)**:
  - Inhale 4s ➔ Hold 4s ➔ Exhale 4s ➔ Rest 4s.
  - Lowers heart rate, stimulates the vagus nerve, and shifts neural processing from the amygdala to the prefrontal cortex.
- **STOP Practice (MBSR)**:
  - **S (Stop)**: Pause physical and mental activities.
  - **T (Take a breath)**: Take a deep, deliberate breath and anchor attention.
  - **O (Observe)**: Notice bodily sensations, emotions, and thoughts without judgment.
  - **P (Proceed)**: Step into the reflection with clear awareness.

## 🌟 Stage 1 & 2: Life Balance (F-SPIRE) & Decision Templates

### Life Balance Dimensions (F-SPIRE)
1. **💰 Financial (財務)**: 4M Money | 4F Fuel your future | 6P PROVE yourself right.
2. **🕊️ Spiritual (靈性)**: 4F Find your purpose | 6P PRAYER always.
3. **💪 Physical (身體)**: 4M Muscle | 6P PUSH yourself when doubted.
4. **📚 Intellectual (智識)**: 4M Mindset | 4F Focus on your present | 6P PROMISE yourself never give up.
5. **🤝 Relational (關係)**: 6P PROUD of who you are.
6. **❤️ Emotional (情緒)**: 4M Mood | 4F Forgive your past | 6P PICK yourself up when you fall.

### Decision Templates
- **🔍 Problem Solving (問題解決)**: Root-cause identification and solution evaluation.
- **🚀 Retrospective (專案復盤)**: Milestone review, team morale, lessons learned, process changes.
- **🎯 Career Decision (職涯決策)**: Opportunity costs, alignment with long-term vision, next steps.
- **📖 Book & Learning (讀書學習)**: Core theories, resonances/critiques, mental model updates, micro-actions.
- **🛡️ Stoic Reflection (斯多葛反思)**: Dichotomy of control, emotional impulse audit, virtue alignment.
- **📅 Weekly Reflection (每週回顧)**: Progress metrics, emotional highlights/lows, time-energy audit, next week goals.

## 💬 Stage 2: ORID Focused Conversation Prompts
1. **O — Objective (客觀事實)**: Observable data, metrics, events, and background facts (No subjective judgments).
2. **R — Reflective (反應感受)**: Emotional tone, physical gut feelings, intuitive associations.
3. **I — Interpretive (詮釋意義)**: Deep root causes, underlying values, insights, and opportunities.
4. **D — Decisional (決定行動)**: Clear, concrete, actionable commitments.

---

## 🏛️ Stage 3: Synthesis & Action Planning

### 1. Pyramid Principle & MECE
- **Key Message (Apex)**: Single overarching, actionable recommendation (linked to D).
- **Supporting Arguments**: 3-4 distinct reasons adhering to MECE (Mutually Exclusive, Collectively Exhaustive).
- **Supporting Facts & Evidence**: Empirical metrics and data points (linked to O).

### 2. PDCA Action Cycle
- **Plan (P)**: Specific objective, target scope, and timeline.
- **Do (D)**: Task owners, responsibilities, and key milestones.
- **Check (C)**: Quantitative KPIs and verification criteria.
- **Act (A)**: Standardization into SOPs or corrective adjustments.

### 3. SMART Goal Criteria
- **Specific (S)**: Crisp, unambiguous outcome.
- **Measurable (M)**: Trackable numbers and acceptance criteria.
- **Achievable (A)**: Feasible within capabilities and resource constraints.
- **Relevant (R)**: Directly aligned with core decision and personal values.
- **Time-bound (T)**: Clear deadlines and review checkpoints.

---

## 🎯 Stage 4: Decision Quality (DQ) Score
Evaluate reflections across four dimensions (1 to 5 scale):
1. **Clarity (清晰度)**: Is the key message crisp and unambiguous?
2. **Evidence (事實證據)**: Is it grounded in objective facts and data?
3. **Insight (洞察深度)**: Did you discover deep root causes rather than superficial symptoms?
4. **Actionability (可執行性)**: Are PDCA and SMART actions concrete and trackable?
- **Composite DQ Score**: `(Clarity + Evidence + Insight + Actionability) / 4`.
